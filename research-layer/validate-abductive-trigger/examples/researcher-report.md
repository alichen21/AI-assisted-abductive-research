## Observation 1: waiting for a milestone is not necessarily novel

The synthetic interview records an agreement without equipment investment, then says, “Keep the possibility of cooperation open; place an order only after testing meets the target.” The issue is not merely whether cooperation exists, but why actual commitment is delayed after expressing intent. [Synthetic source passages](iteration-project/empirical/raw-data/source-demo.md)

The first explanation covered signals and interpretation but not waiting. Following the researcher's suggestion about preserving future options, the subsequent synthetic explanation connects uncertainty, milestones, and deferrable investment. Both observations are compatible with it. [Follow-up synthetic explanation](iteration-project/literature/L-DEMO-02.md)

The earlier lens-specific noncoverage therefore becomes bounded coverage by an examined adjacent explanation. It does not establish every actor motive or a universal causal relationship.
