# Current analytical outcome

The [case progress report](AI_Abductive_Case_Provisional_Findings_Zhang_Xue_EN_v2.docx) describes how theory–evidence comparisons redirected the inquiry. Its outcome is a refined working research question, rather than a confirmed abductive trigger.

Existing theory could accommodate different levels of enthusiasm for inventing, founding, and developing a venture. A preference for making motorcycles, combined with reluctance toward entrepreneurship, was therefore insufficient to establish a theoretical discrepancy. Observations of continuing involvement in financing, organizing, control, and growth focused attention on how the founder understands that organizational work.

The report presents the AI's revised working question as:

> How do founders form, delimit, or reject an entrepreneurial identity when venture creation serves a valued maker identity and entails sustained engagement in financing, organizing, and venture growth?

The question leaves open whether entrepreneurial identity becomes central, remains secondary, or is resisted. Venture activity alone does not resolve that issue. The report also leaves open competing interpretations of continuity and change in the meaning of achievement.

The literature comparison used selected full-text passages, abstracts, and identified earlier versions. It supports a bounded comparison, not an exhaustive claim of theoretical novelty or a demonstrated effect of AI assistance. The report is an analytical account rather than a complete execution log.
