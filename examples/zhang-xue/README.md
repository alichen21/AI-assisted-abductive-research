## Scope

The initial hunch concerned repeated venture creation alongside a stated preference for making motorcycles over entrepreneurship. The inquiry examined whether entrepreneurial roles could serve a valued rider or maker identity, using entrepreneurial identity formation as its main lens. This was a retrospective exercise that began with the researcher's hunch and preliminary observations.

The report uses selected literature and archived observations. It is an illustrative inquiry, not a controlled evaluation of AI effectiveness. The accompanying source index documents this supplied collection; it is not a complete record of all research episodes, coding, or researcher feedback. The 0.8.7 suite in this repository is the distributed toolkit, not a verified statement of the version used at every stage of the case.

## Status

The current outcome is a more focused research question. A theoretical discrepancy and a confirmed abductive trigger have not been established. Identity centrality, secondary identification, and resistance to a founder identity remain open possibilities.
