# The ZX-Moto effect: over 5,000 motorcycles sold in less than 3 weeks

## The ZX-Moto Effect: Over 5,000 Motorcycles Sold in Less Than 3 Weeks

At the penultimate round of the Superbike World Championship, held at the Portimao circuit, the Chinese manufacturer ZX-Moto made quite an impression by securing a historic one-two finish.

Credit goes to Valentin Debise and the Evan Bros team, who, riding the 820 RR, swept aside the competition from Yamaha, Kawasaki, Triumph, Ducati, Honda, MV Agusta, and QJ.

- Moto - News Richard Hammond: Ducati Panigale V2 or... ZXMoto 820RR?

For the Asian brand, it was a milestone that was, in some ways, unexpected, and back home, the news of the triumph was met with a response that was nothing short of surprising.

According to reports from Assen, in less than three weeks, ZX-Moto received orders for over 5,000 820 RRs. Sales are expected to reach around 5,600 units.

Furthermore, according to data reported by Dorna, the response on social media regarding the live broadcast of Race 1 and Race 2 on the Douyin channel was particularly significant. Saturday’s race was viewed by 6,376,090 fans, while Sunday’s race drew 9,314,560 viewers.
