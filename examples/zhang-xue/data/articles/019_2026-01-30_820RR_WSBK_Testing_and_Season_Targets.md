After our previous two features on WSBK's Jerez winter test, many readers asked about the Zhang Xue 820RR's test results. In fact, the Zhang Xue factory team did not attend the Jerez test. Instead, it completed several rounds of private testing in an environment offering greater flexibility and freedom from outside interference.

These tests were conducted in relative secrecy. This article brings together and assesses the limited, fragmentary information disclosed to overseas media. Since the factory team's official announcement at the Milan motorcycle show in November 2025, it has completed at least four rounds of testing:

The first shakedown took place in early November, immediately after the Milan show, when factory rider Caricasulo briefly tested at Italy's Pomposa circuit. He completed a few laps of the small, 1.6-kilometer track. The motorcycle was still entirely in stock form, allowing the rider to familiarize himself with it for the first time.

The second test took place at Italy's Vallelunga circuit. Factory rider Debise covered it on his personal channel, DebiseTV. According to him, the body panels, clutch, and other components were still stock. Initial race conversions had been completed on more universally adaptable components such as the exhaust, handlebars, and ECU, while bespoke racing components were still under development.

Although the video showed one red and one white 820RR in the pit garage, the two factory riders took turns testing only the red motorcycle. Debise explained that this allowed them to concentrate all their efforts on developing a single bike. In the video, he also suggested ergonomic changes to handlebar height, fuel-tank shape, and the seat. These improvements should become possible once the custom components are finished.

Together, the two factory riders subjected the motorcycle to more than 100 kilometers of intensive testing at Vallelunga. They tried several setup and configuration combinations, both to collect data and to find settings best suited to their riding styles.

The third test was completed in early January at Spain's Circuito de Andalucía, and a fourth was completed in late January. The team's social-media account recently posted an on-track testing video, which Zhang Xue also shared. It was probably filmed during the fourth test. The video shows a motorcycle with a white stock fuel tank and newly fitted gray race fairings. Apart from the tank, it has shed the appearance of a road bike and looks almost ready for competition.

In another video posted by factory rider Caricasulo, we noticed that he was riding an 820RR with a red stock tank and black race fairings during this test. It appears that the race conversions of both the red and white bikes are largely complete. The riders no longer need to share one motorcycle, allowing each machine to be modified and set up for its rider's needs and habits. Reportedly, during the three-day Andalucía test, each rider completed more than 200 kilometers of intensive testing per day.

Fabio Evangelista, head of the Evan Bros team, the Zhang Xue factory team's Italian partner, shared information about preparations and targets for the new season in an interview with Italian outlet Corsedimoto.

"We sent our very best technical staff to China to provide support: technical director Mauro Pellegrini, engine specialist Jeremy Togni, and mechanic Mauro D'Ascenzo. They worked shoulder to shoulder with the Chinese engineers, tackling problems on site, and the Chinese team has shown tremendous enthusiasm for the partnership. Our work is currently focused primarily on the engine, with the central aim of finding the right balance between reliability and performance. The motorcycle still uses the stock power unit. The Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM) is currently conducting model homologation at our headquarters, which also serves, to some extent, as ZX Moto's overseas R&D division."

"We are trying some small but important modifications. At the start of the season, we will race a motorcycle that is close to stock specification. At the same time, a complete competition package covering both engine and chassis setup is under development. It is expected to enter service in the second half of the season, bringing a major step forward in performance."

On the targets for the 2026 season, he said: "Our goal at this stage is clear: to finish in the points. It does not matter if we cannot achieve that immediately at the Phillip Island opener; we will continue to make steady progress. A top-15 finish would be excellent, and reaching the top ten would be an even more significant breakthrough. Step by step, we will get there. This March, ZX Moto will hold a new-model launch in China, introducing a factory-team replica that will officially enter the Chinese market. Looking ahead to 2027, we hope to see more Zhang Xue 820RRs competing together in WSSP and other national championships. That is what we are hoping for, and it will make the whole team proud."

While the factory team has been busy with preseason testing, the arrival of two sponsors, Eastroc Super Drink and Bitubo suspension, has also attracted widespread online attention. Eastroc's involvement seems largely attributable to online appeals from fans, while Bitubo's signing reflects strong overseas interest in the Zhang Xue brand. The sponsors shown in the image above have not all been individually confirmed, but the overall picture indicates that both the Chinese and overseas sponsors are leaders in their fields—no small achievement for a motorcycle manufacturer established only a little over a year ago.

The new WSBK season opens at Australia's Phillip Island circuit on February 20–22, during China's Lunar New Year holiday. Many motorcycle enthusiasts will no doubt stay up late to watch the Zhang Xue 820RR's international racing debut. Before that, on February 16 and 17—Lunar New Year's Eve and New Year's Day for us—WSBK will hold its final official preseason test at Phillip Island. The Zhang Xue factory team is very likely to make an early appearance there. We will keep watching and bring you first-hand updates, so remember to follow us.
