# [China Motorcycle Expo] Former Kove Founder Zhang Xue Returns with ZX Moto and Launches the Four-Cylinder ZX-500RR Sportbike

Former Kove founder Zhang Xue has returned with an all-new brand, "Zhang Xue Motorcycles" (ZX Moto). At the 2024 China Motorcycle Expo (Chongqing Motorcycle/Electric Bicycle Exhibition), he unveiled the brand's first model, the four-cylinder ZX-500RR sportbike. With a class-leading 85 horsepower, extensive electronic rider aids, and top-tier equipment, it officially joins the "four-cylinder battle" between Chinese and Japanese brands.

### ZX-500RR: Japanese Sportbike DNA and an Aggressive Stance

The ZX-500RR's styling draws on the essence of Japanese sportbikes. Sharp body lines combine Japanese sportbike elements to create a dynamic visual impact. The front features twin "eagle-eye" headlights with LED daytime-running-light strips, while carbon-fiber-pattern winglets sit on both sides of the nose. At the rear, an additional Z-shaped lighting unit accompanies the brake light to make the bike more recognizable.

A sportbike style with a hint of both Yamaha and Honda.

The side profile is clean and crisp, without excessive embellishment or contrived bodywork, giving it something of a 1990s sportbike feel.

Alongside the LED brake light, a Z-shaped lighting unit adds a distinctly Kawasaki-like feel.

### ZX-500RR: Class-Leading Horsepower and Lightweight Design

Power comes from a 500cc liquid-cooled DOHC inline-four engine producing a maximum of 85PS at 13,500rpm and 46Nm at 12,000rpm. Official figures put top speed at around 240km/h and 0–100km/h acceleration at 3.4 seconds.

The chassis uses a lightweight, one-piece tubular-steel trellis frame paired with an aluminum-alloy single-sided rear swingarm to reduce weight. The display bike also has CNC-machined aluminum-alloy upper and lower triple clamps. It is not yet clear whether the final production version will change, but the current wet weight is just 168kg, lighter than the Kawasaki ZX-4R, another four-cylinder model.

The bike has a 500cc liquid-cooled DOHC inline-four engine. Judging by the engine cases on both sides, it appears to be based on the Honda CBR400RR engine.

Even the stock exhaust has the look of an aftermarket system.

A single-sided rear swingarm is paired with a six-spoke wheel.

### Advanced Lightweight-Class Equipment for Maximum Handling Enjoyment

Braking is handled by opposed four-piston "Blue Spot"-style radial front calipers and a single-piston sliding rear caliper. Suspension consists of an inverted fork with adjustable preload and damping, plus an adjustable rear monoshock. A concealed linear steering damper is also fitted to improve high-speed stability.

The ZX-500RR is equally generous with electronics. It has a TFT instrument display measuring more than 5 inches. Alongside the now-standard dual-channel ABS and traction-control system, it offers a one-way electronic quickshifter and advanced features such as cornering ABS and launch control, taking sporting performance and handling enjoyment to the limit.

The TFT display is larger than 5 inches, with a simple, intuitive interface.

The CNC-machined aluminum-alloy upper triple clamp looks well finished; the inverted fork has adjustable preload and damping.

The brakes use a radial master cylinder.

The opposed four-piston radial calipers closely resemble Yamaha's "Blue Spot" design.

### ZXJC ZX-500RR Key Specifications

- Length × width × height: 2005 × 706 × 1125mm

- Wheelbase: 1390mm

- Seat height: 795mm

- Weight: 168kg (wet)

- Engine: Liquid-cooled, four-stroke, DOHC inline-four, four valves

- Maximum power: 85PS/13,500rpm

- Maximum torque: 46Nm/12,000rpm

- Fuel tank capacity: 15L

- Transmission: Six-speed sequential

- Brakes: Front = twin discs; rear = single disc

- Tires: Front = 120/70ZR17; rear = 180/55ZR17
