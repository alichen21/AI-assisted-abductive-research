## ZX Moto Reaches the Top of a World-Class Competition: How Can China's Motorcycle Industry Catch the Leading Nations?

[Caixin] From March 27 to 29, the middleweight races at the Portuguese round of the Superbike World Championship (WSBK) saw intense competition. French rider Valentin Debise, number 53, crossed the line first in both races, sweeping the round's victories.

The wins ignited enthusiasm in China's motorcycle community. The spotlight fell both on the results and on the racing machine, modified from Chinese brand ZX Moto's production 820RR.

This was the first middleweight-class victory for a Chinese factory motorcycle team since entering WSBK. Its rivals included international giants Ducati, Honda, Kawasaki, Yamaha, and Triumph. Top-level motorcycle racing has always showcased the world's leading brands, making ZX Moto's victory a milestone.
