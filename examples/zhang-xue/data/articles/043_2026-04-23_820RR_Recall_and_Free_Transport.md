# ZX Moto Recalls All Delivered 820RRs for Remedial Work; Stores Notify Owners Individually and Arrange Free Transport

> Source: [Sichuan Online / The Cover (Li Maojia)](https://sichuan.scol.com.cn/ggxw/202604/83243409.html)

- Sichuan > Rolling News > Article

## ZX Moto Urgently Recalls 286 Championship-Model 820RR Motorcycles; Owners Are Being Notified Individually

The Cover reporter Li Maojia

At 15:19 on April 22, ZX Moto's official WeChat account published an 820RR recall notice, initiating a comprehensive recall and remedial program for a vehicle fault. All 286 delivered motorcycles are involved.

The notice offers two solutions depending on vehicle condition: returns or replacements, and free-maintenance compensation, aimed at protecting riders' safety.

On visiting a store, The Cover learned that staff were contacting affected owners individually and would arrange motorcycle transport. Official customer service also said owners could ask stores to perform other relevant technical inspections if needed.

ZX Moto publishes an 820RR recall notice.

Stores: Notify owners individually and transport recalled motorcycles free

The notice says the recall arose from a vehicle fault. On April 16, an 820RR in Yinchuan, Ningxia, suffered a cracked crankcase. The damaged bike returned to the factory on April 21. Dismantling revealed the cause: an incorrectly installed O-ring on the pressure-relief valve. After some use, the valve loosened, allowing oil pressure to escape, causing insufficient lubrication and damage to the connecting-rod bearing.

Owners are therefore asked to stop using the 820RR and contact an authorized store to have it transported there for remedial work.

The company offers two solutions. First, if the pressure-relief valve has already loosened and has play, the owner may choose a return or replacement. Second, if the valve remains secure with no play and only the O-ring needs replacement, the owner receives two free maintenance services.

About an hour after the notice, The Cover visited ZX Moto's Liangjiang New Area store. No owners had yet arrived to ask about the recall.

Staff said they received the recall notice immediately and were contacting owners individually as required, urgently telling them to stop using their motorcycles. "Before closing today, we will check each salesperson's completion of owner notifications."

Regarding inspection transport, the employee said the store would arrange it: "Depending on the circumstances, we may collect it ourselves or call Lalamove to bring it back."

"In addition to correcting the fault specified in the recall notice, we will conduct a comprehensive safety inspection."

On further details, staff said the official announcement should be taken as authoritative and they could not comment further for now.

ZX Moto stores contact affected owners individually.

Official customer service: Owners may also request other technical inspections

The reporter then called ZX Moto's national hotline. Customer service clarified transport arrangements: owners can contact their nearest authorized store. If it has towing capacity, staff will collect the bike; otherwise, owners may arrange Lalamove transport to the store, with the company covering all transport costs.

Customer service emphasized that testing had so far identified only the pressure-relief valve assembly fault as affecting safe riding. "Owners may also request other technical inspections if needed."

Zhang also posted a video about the issue that day.

He said: "The Yinchuan 820RR has returned. We ultimately found that the pressure-relief valve's O-ring should normally sit in its corresponding groove. That is the correct assembly. Why was it installed incorrectly? Our earlier design lacked this baffle. We added one in an optimization to stop bubbles forming. But through our own failure to train staff properly, it was installed backward in production. There was then no clearance groove for the O-ring, allowing loosening. Once loose, pressure could not be maintained; it continually leaked pressure, drew in air, and damaged the bearing."

He reminded owners again: "We have delivered 286 820RRs. Please stop using them and contact a ZX Moto authorized store to transport the vehicle there for remedial work."

In the 2026 Superbike World Championship season, ZX Moto's competition machine is derived from the 820RR. Rider number 53, Valentin Debise, took the 820RR-RS to victory in both races at the Portuguese round.
