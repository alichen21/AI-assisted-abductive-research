# ZX Moto Recalls 286 820RR Motorcycles over Risk of Engine Seizure

> Source: [Jiemian News](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14965692.html)

According to the State Administration for Market Regulation website, Chongqing Zhangxue Motorcycle Industry Co., Ltd. recently filed a recall plan with the administration under the Regulations on the Administration of Recalls of Defective Automotive Products and their implementing measures.

Recall No. S2026M0061V: Effective immediately, the company recalls 286 model-year 2026 820RR motorcycles produced between April 3 and April 21, 2026.

In the affected vehicles, incorrect installation of the engine pressure-relief valve O-ring can compress, deform, and damage the seal, loosening the valve assembly. The resulting oil leakage and insufficient lubrication may damage connecting-rod bearings, crack the crankcase, or seize the engine. In extreme cases, the rider could crash, posing a safety hazard.

The company will inspect affected motorcycles free of charge. If the pressure-relief valve has not loosened, the O-ring will be replaced. If it has loosened, owners may choose a return or replacement to eliminate the safety hazard.
