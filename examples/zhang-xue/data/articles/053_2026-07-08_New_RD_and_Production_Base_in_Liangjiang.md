## ZX Moto and CMCU Engineering Sign Agreement for a Motorcycle R&D and Production Base in Chongqing's Liangjiang New Area

Science and Technology Daily reporter Yong Li; correspondent Xie Li

On July 8, the reporter learned from Chongqing's Liangjiang New Area that ZX Moto and CMCU Engineering had recently signed a cooperation agreement in Chongqing to jointly build a new R&D and production base there.

The planned base will have annual capacity of 500,000 motorcycles, combining large-scale production, smart manufacturing, and premium brand displays. Its production processes will accommodate product iterations over the next decade. It is a key strategic move in ZX Moto's effort to build a world-class motorcycle brand and will effectively meet global demand across its range.

ZX Moto has won several international races this year, returning China's long-quiet motorcycle industry to the spotlight. Zhang disclosed on social media that the company produced over 10,000 units in June.

Strong demand has accelerated the new base's development. In June, ZX Moto spent RMB 48.48 million to acquire a new production site in Liangjiang New Area to expand capacity further.

CMCU Engineering Party secretary and chairman Zhao Yongbo said the company would assemble an expert team to tailor an industry-leading modern smart-motorcycle manufacturing park, helping Chongqing's motorcycle industry develop into a higher-end cluster.
