# Kove Founder Zhang Xue Announces His Resignation

> Source: [Autohome Chejiahao, "Four Wheels, Two Wheels"](https://chejiahao.autohome.com.cn/info/14728401?isfrom=m)

- Chejiahao

- Brand analysis

- Article

Has Kove Motorcycles boss Zhang Xue resigned? On the very first day of March, news of Zhang's resignation was already spreading across the internet. For riders who like Kove, this news really is quite a blow.

I believe that perhaps nine out of every ten people who buy Kove motorcycles do so because of Zhang Xue's approach to building bikes. As Kove's first motorcycle, the 500X caused a tremendous stir in the market as soon as it launched in 2019.

Even today, this emerging Chinese brand remains synonymous with a focus on performance and the pursuit of the ultimate. I would go so far as to say that Kove's rapid growth in such a short time is inseparable from Zhang Xue.

Apart from Kove's Zhang Xue, most internet users probably cannot name the founders behind today's major Chinese motorcycle brands. In many people's eyes, a large part of Kove's rapid growth can be attributed to Zhang's personal charisma.

The line in this resignation letter, "In the future, we will be friends, and also competitors," leaves plenty of room for speculation. After all, motorcycles are as important as life itself to Zhang Xue, and I believe he will remain active in this field.

Finally, unless Zhang Xue himself confirms the authenticity of the resignation letter above, all of this remains rumor. Please follow the story rationally.
