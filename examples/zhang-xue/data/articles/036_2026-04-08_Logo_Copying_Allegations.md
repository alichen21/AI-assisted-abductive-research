# ZX Moto Logo Accused of Copying; Design Company Says a Statement Will Follow

> Source: [The Beijing News, Shell Finance (Lin Zi)](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/1775632865129902.html)

The Beijing News, Shell Finance (reporter Lin Zi): On April 8, social-media users alleged that ZX Moto's logo copied others, closely resembling logos of ZETA, Zhongle International, and several other companies: "The geometric shapes and directions of the lines are very similar." The reporter noted that Zhang had previously said the overall design cost about RMB 400,000, while the central graphic logo was "provided free."

Baton's website displays ZX Moto's brand-design scheme. Screenshot from the company's website.

The logo was designed by Hangzhou Baton Brand Design. Its website presents the design approach. Other clients include Xiangpiaopiao, Goodme, CoCo, and Jiedian.

On April 8, the reporter sought confirmation from Zhang, who had not responded by publication. Hangzhou Baton Brand Design said it had noticed the comments and would issue a statement later.
