# ZX Moto Logo in Copying Dispute; Design Company Responds: Legal Action Is Welcome

> Source: [Jiemian News](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14254256.html)

The design company has formally responded after ZX Moto's logo became embroiled in allegations of copying.

On the evening of April 14, Hangzhou Baton Brand Design Co., Ltd. issued a statement saying it had recently noticed online debate about the logo. Baton said it had created the design independently, and that the related trademark had been submitted to and approved for registration by the China National Intellectual Property Administration.

The company said it welcomed and supported any party believing its rights had been infringed to seek a judicial resolution.

ZX Moto attracted public attention after two consecutive middleweight victories at the Portuguese round of the Superbike World Championship. On April 8, social-media users alleged that its logo copied others, closely resembling those of ZETA, Zhongle International, and several other companies: "The geometric shapes and line directions are very similar."

Some independent-media posts also claimed the logo closely resembled existing marks, being "almost identical in graphic structure, line direction, and overall form, with only minor detail changes and an extremely high degree of similarity."

Founder Zhang Xue previously said in a video that a Hangzhou design company created the logo as part of a RMB 400,000 package including visual identity and store presentation.

Zhang also interacted with Baton on Douyin. In his comments section, Baton explained that blue was chosen because Zhang came from a background as a professional, composed rider, technically focused on ultimate motorcycle performance and lightweight development. Blue would convey a professional, premium brand tone and distinguish it from competitors mostly using warm colors. "Both sides agreed."

Tianyancha shows that Hangzhou Baton Brand Design Co., Ltd. was established on July 4, 2008, with registered capital of RMB 5 million. Its legal representative is Zhou Minbin, and it was formerly called Hangzhou Shunchang Technology Co., Ltd. Its business scope includes corporate brand design, commercial brand planning, corporate image planning, commercial brand management, marketing planning, multimedia design, computer-network technology development and consulting, and graphic design.

Jiemian News noted that Baton presents the ZX Moto logo's design story on its website: "In 2024, when the brand was newly established, Zhang faced an urgent problem: how could a new brand communicate more effectively with consumers and approach the world with a more international image? He chose Baton to build a systematic identity around three dimensions—brand visual design, brand spaces, and brand culture—creating a complete brand system, telling a Chinese brand's story, and bridging communication between Zhang's brand and global users."

Baton's services included motorcycle logo design, motorcycle brand design, visual identity design, and brand-space design, with a creation date of July 25, 2024.

According to Baton: "The logo centers on ZX, the initials of Zhang Xue, reconstructed with crisp geometric lines. Its sharp diagonal forms convey racetrack speed and the passion and professionalism of motorcycle sport." It adds: "Centered on precise angles of 28 and 117 degrees and refined to the utmost, rigorous geometric proportions give the ZX Moto mark a sense of racetrack speed and professional quality."

Beyond ZX Moto, Baton's clients include Purcotton, Goodme, Youkeshu, and CoCo.

Notably, during a livestream by his mentor "Brother Ya," Zhang Jixing, Zhang heard a rumor that Neta Auto spent RMB 500 million designing a logo and said: "RMB 500 million to design a logo—certain death... A business must first survive. Money should go into R&D and products." He also said Kove's logo had cost RMB 600 from a Taobao designer.

Former Neta CEO Zhang Yong had already responded to that rumor. On April 18, 2025, he wrote on WeChat Moments that Neta's fountain logo had been in use since June 1, 2018, and its design cost no more than RMB 1 million. He said Hozon's branding and product promotion over those seven years had centered on "Neta" and the fountain mark. His actual livestream words were: "The money spent producing the logo is no less than RMB 500 million, including all official seals, logos, and renovations at hundreds of domestic and overseas outlets."

Zhang Yong stressed that he meant Hozon's total spending over seven years on brand promotion, marketing communications, market development, and channel building around the Neta name was at least RMB 500 million. A hasty name change would cause substantial waste and losses. He did not mean designing a single logo cost RMB 500 million.
