## [Business Circle] Zhang Xue: Twenty Years of Devotion to a Motorcycle Dream

[Caixin Data Special Report] On March 28–29, French rider Valentin Debise rode an 820RR produced by Chinese company ZX Moto to victory in both middleweight-class races at the Portuguese round of the Superbike World Championship (WSBK).

In Race 1, Debise finished a full four seconds ahead of the runner-up, leaving behind top international motorcycle teams including Ducati, Honda, Kawasaki, Yamaha, and Triumph.

This was the first middleweight victory since Chinese motorcycle companies began fielding WSBK teams. ZX Moto had only just turned two years old. Standing out among so many giants was no small achievement for such a young company.
