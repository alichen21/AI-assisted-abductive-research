# ZX Moto Wins at WSBK's Czech Round: Five Victories This Season and Sights on the Overall Title

> Source: [Jiemian News](https://www.jiemian.com/article/14440167.html)

According to CCTV News, on May 17 local time, French rider Valentin Debise of Chinese manufacturer ZX Moto won Race 2 in the WorldSSP class at the Czech round of the 2026 Superbike World Championship (WSBK). It was the team's second sweep of both races at one round this season, after Portugal, and its fifth victory with Debise.

Debise had previously won four WSBK races on the Zhang Xue 820RR: both Portuguese races on March 28 and 29, Race 1 in Hungary on May 2, and Race 1 in the Czech Republic on May 16.

The Czech venue, Most, is widely called "braking hell" by riders. Its 4.212 kilometers pack in 21 corners and ten extreme braking points, with few short straights and many consecutive blind corners. Riders must go from rapid acceleration to hard deceleration in seconds, severely testing braking, stable power delivery, precise chassis setup, riding rhythm, and line choice.

The circuit offers little margin for error, with loss of control and collisions easily arising in intense competition, making this victory particularly valuable.

In the round's first race on May 16, Debise was penalized for an infringement in free practice and forced to start the second race from sixth, an unfavorable position. After the start, he patiently studied riders' lines rather than attempting reckless overtakes, controlled his rhythm precisely, and began a series of attacks on lap seven.

With three laps remaining, tensions rose, and several riders, including Masia, lost control and slid off during the fierce contest.

Debise traded the lead repeatedly with Yamaha's Turkish rider Öncü. In the middle-to-late portion of the final lap, he made the decisive pass and closed the line to win. ZX Moto's other rider, Caricasulo, finished tenth.

Afterward, Zhang said excitedly that the victory was particularly meaningful and would greatly boost their points: "Five wins now—we can dream of the season championship!"

ZX Moto has now swept both races in Portugal and the Czech Republic, adding a Hungarian win for five WSBK race victories this season. Debise's large points haul from the Czech double lifted him to second in the riders' standings. ZX Moto remains third among manufacturers, behind only established international brands Ducati and Yamaha, with a possible challenge for this season's overall WSBK title. The season has twelve rounds; round six at Aragón, Spain, is scheduled for May 29–31.

Established by the International Motorcycling Federation in 1988, WSBK is a world-class championship distinguished by motorcycles modified within limits from production models. Known as "a motorcycle show on the racetrack," it is an important stage for manufacturers to demonstrate production-bike technology.

WorldSSP requires bikes derived from commercially available production models, reaching 240–260km/h and testing engine, frame, and electronic-system capability. Ducati, Yamaha, Honda, and Kawasaki long dominated the class.

ZX Moto's 820RR-RS uses its independently developed 819cc inline-three engine. It has achieved independent advances in power delivery, lightweight design, brake-system setup, and track-adapted chassis design, allowing it to suit different circuits and withstand intense, prolonged competition, demonstrating Chinese motorcycle manufacturing's strength and technical confidence.

After the May 16 race, Debise said: "Many thanks to ZX Moto. Once again today they gave me a potent weapon." He was pleased that more Chinese fans were following and supporting him and hoped to race in China one day. Zhang also addressed him: "Your next task is to train a Chinese rider even better than you, and I will look after you in retirement."

This March, Debise rode ZX Moto's number 53 820RR-RS to two middleweight wins at WSBK's Portimão round in Portugal, making history for Chinese motorcycle brands and breaking a monopoly of more than thirty years by European, American, and Japanese brands such as Ducati and Yamaha.

Since then, both the company and Zhang himself have attracted growing attention.

At the Canton Fair in mid-April, first-time exhibitor ZX Moto's stand became the busiest place in its exhibition area from opening day. Foreign-trade department head Huang Qin said: "It has been extremely busy these two days. Over 5,000 buyers came specifically for our brand, asking to become local agents and place orders directly. We received orders for over a thousand bikes."

Staff also told media that orders multiplied after the victories. The championship-model 820RR was booked through October, while the 820X and 820R would launch later this year.

Official information shows that the company launched the 500RR and 500F in 2025, selling a cumulative 25,000 units. Reservations for the 2026 500RR and 820RR opened on March 21, 2026; by March 25, firm orders totaled 5,543.

At the 2026 partner conference, Zhang disclosed 2025 output value of RMB 750 million, R&D investment of RMB 69.58 million, an R&D-to-sales ratio of 9.33%, and a loss of RMB 22.78 million.

Tianyancha shows that Chongqing Zhangxue Motorcycle Industry Co., Ltd. was established in April 2024 with registered capital of RMB 65.4 million. Zhang is its legal representative and holds 73.3945%. It specializes in four- and three-cylinder supersports, with 2025 sales exceeding 25,000. In early March, ZX Moto completed Series A financing at a post-money valuation of about RMB 1.09 billion.
