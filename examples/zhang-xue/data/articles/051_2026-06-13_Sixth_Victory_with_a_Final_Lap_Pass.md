# A Final-Lap Pass: ZX Moto Wins Again!

> Source: [Beijing Daily / Jingbao Sports](https://news.bjd.com.cn/2026/06/13/11805306.shtml)

## A Final-Lap Pass: ZX Moto Wins Again!

Race 1 in the WorldSSP class at the Superbike World Championship's Emilia-Romagna round in Italy has concluded. ZX Moto rider Debise passed on the final lap to win. It was the brand's sixth WSBK race trophy this season, following two in Portugal, one in Hungary, and two in the Czech Republic.

Several factors underpinned the sixth victory. First was Debise's ability: after repeated unsuccessful inside passing attempts, he forced his way inside at turns 11–12 on the last lap and firmly defended the lead, displaying exceptional composure and judgment. Second was the motorcycle's performance. The 820RR-RS has an independently developed inline-three engine, and its lightweight chassis gives advantages in corner-exit acceleration and terminal speed on straights, providing the hardware for overtaking. Third was teamwork: precise tire management maintained enough late-race grip and acceleration to enable the final-lap pass.

According to the Emilia-Romagna schedule, ZX Moto will enter Race 2 at 21:15 Beijing time on June 14.
