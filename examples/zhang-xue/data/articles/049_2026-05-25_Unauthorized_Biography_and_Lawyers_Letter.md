Zhang Xue Issues Lawyer's Letter Saying He Has Never Published a Book; Store Customer Service Says the Title Has Been Removed

Source: Ziniu News

2026-05-25 13:30:00

On May 24, ZX Moto issued a statement about a book recently appearing on the market, Brave Dream Chaser: Zhang Xue's Extreme Passion and Against-the-Odds Life (ISBN: 9787559692696), compiled and written by Zhang Xiaoping. Zhang Xue said he had never authorized Zhang Xiaoping or Beijing United Publishing Co., Ltd. to compile, publish, or distribute it. He said its unauthorized use of his name, personal experiences, and private information constituted serious infringement. ZX Moto attached a lawyer's letter dated May 20. Chongqing Kunyuan Hengtai Law Firm said Zhang Xiaoping had written the book based on Zhang Xue's life without authorization or permission. It covered more than twenty years from his adolescence through the recent founding of ZX Moto and participation in the 2026 Superbike World Championship. Some information had not been publicly disclosed and was private. Zhang also posted a video on his personal account, "Zhang Xue's Motorcycles": "After we won, many publishers invited us to produce a book. We declined all requests because we do not think we are qualified yet. But colleagues saw a similar book online. Let me state that it has nothing to do with me. Our colleagues sent a lawyer's letter, and the other side did not respond. I told colleagues that if we have a chance of winning, we should sue directly. Any damages received will go to charity." According to Publishers Magazine's May 18 introduction, the book was published by Beijing United Publishing and planned by Beijing Jieteng Culture as "the first authoritative, documented biography of Zhang Xue." Notably, its Douban Books page has now been amended: the subtitle reads "The First Zhang Xue Biography Pretending to Be Based on Official Records," and the description says, "The contents were not authorized by Mr. Zhang Xue and constitute an unlawful, infringing publication." Previously, the book sold for RMB 49.8 at Dangdang's Jieteng Culture store and Wenhaixuan Books specialty store. Both now show it as "out of stock." Asked about it, Jieteng customer service confirmed it had been removed. The company's WeChat promotional post has also been deleted. Yangtse Evening Post / Ziniu News reporter Shen Zhao.
