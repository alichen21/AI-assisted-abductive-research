# ZX Moto Faces Complaint over Ban on Novice 820RR Buyers; Zhang Xue: I Do Not Want That 10% of Sales

> Source: [Jiemian News](https://m.jiemian.com/article/14199053.html)

ZX Moto's 820RR-RS won twice in the WorldSSP class at the Portuguese round of the Superbike World Championship (WSBK), sending consumer interest soaring. At the same time, a user complained to market regulators about its rule against selling to novice riders.

The 820RR supersport opened for reservations late last month. It produces up to 135 horsepower, has a curb weight of 193 kilograms, accelerates from 0–100km/h in 2.81 seconds, and costs RMB 43,800–61,980.

The reservation poster states that the 820RR range is too powerful for beginners to handle. For safety, anyone with less than one year of motorcycle-riding experience is prohibited from buying it. If such a user obtains one from a dealer, they may report the sale to the factory and receive RMB 5,000 after verification.

A video posted by Chongqing ZX Moto founder Zhang Xue subsequently circulated online, showing that a user had complained to market regulators because the large-displacement 820RR on presale could not be purchased by novices with less than one year's experience.

In the video, an employee says the market supervision authority contacted the company that day, saying the ban on consumers who had held licenses for under a year purchasing the 820RR violated fair-trading principles and requesting rectification. "If we do not rectify it, we may be penalized."

Responding to the rule, Zhang said: "I hope a few fewer people die." He acknowledged that it would affect performance—"at least 10% fewer sales"—but immediately added: "I do not want that 10%. The company will not die without it.

On April 1, the Ministry of Public Security's Traffic Management Bureau posted on its official Weibo account: Zhang would rather sell 10% fewer bikes than let novices with less than one year's experience buy large-displacement machines. A helmet and protective gear are his standard professional riding equipment, while the "novice ban" represents the safety threshold he sets for fellow riders. True passion requires knowing limits. Speed can stir the blood, but responsibility alone allows that passion to endure.

Banyuetan wrote that the rule comes against a backdrop of some riders' enthusiasm for noisy street riding and an almost complete lack of safety education. Some consumers chase displacement and horsepower while ignoring basic riding competence. Zhang's "novice ban" reflects an entrepreneur's respect for life and also issues of civility and order that China's motorcycle market must confront.

According to New Yellow River, Chongqing Liangjiang New Area's Market Supervision Administration said on April 1 that the matter had been properly resolved. "It was a misunderstanding. We did not issue a rectification notice. Because someone had complained, we contacted them to understand the situation. They thought we intended to impose penalties. We have since clarified this."

After ZX Moto became famous through its racing victory, orders surged. Zhang told media on March 30 that orders had grown explosively: 820RR sales rose 200% and 500RR sales nearly 100%. His targets for 2026 are 60,000 units sold and an output value of RMB 1.8 billion, requiring a leap that doubles both sales and output value.

Sales figures published on ZX Moto's official WeChat account show that the 500RR topped the individual-model retail ranking for two-wheeled fuel-powered motorcycles in February 2026, selling 1,353 units. It also ranked first in January, with 1,905. In the 2025 brand sales rankings, ZX Moto sold 22,665 units, placing among China's top eight.

Zhang himself has also attracted enthusiastic fans. Third-party data show that his Douyin account, "Zhang Xue's Motorcycles," had 1.478 million followers on March 28. By Jiemian News' publication on April 2, it had 3.331 million, an increase of nearly two million.
