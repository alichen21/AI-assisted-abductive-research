## ZX Moto Receives HongShan Investment at a RMB 6 Billion Post-Money Valuation

On August 13, The Paper learned that ZX Moto had received exclusive investment from HongShan, formerly Sequoia China. People familiar with the matter said the investment was RMB 150 million at a post-money valuation of RMB 6 billion.

The parties will cooperate closely on corporate governance, talent development, industry resources, and globalization. ZX Moto will continue focusing on engine, vehicle, and product R&D and racing validation. HongShan will support long-term development through its governance experience, talent networks, industrial ecosystem, and global resources.

HongShan partner Su Kai said what truly impressed the firm was Zhang's almost obsessive pursuit of product excellence. His lasting love of motorcycles and racing gives him user understanding and a willingness to invest for the long term. "We believe world-class products often emerge from an uncompromising pursuit of the ultimate. HongShan wants to accompany entrepreneurs like this over the long term, challenging world-class products and racing arenas together."

Zhang said HongShan's arrival gave ZX Moto another long-term partner. The team would keep improving engines and complete motorcycles, validating them on track, and listening to genuine user feedback. Chinese high-performance motorcycles have an opportunity to win global users; that requires patience and deserves sustained investment.

Established on April 2, 2024, Chongqing Zhangxue Motorcycle Industry Co., Ltd. had previously raised two rounds. In July 2024, Gaoxin Capital's Shanghai Gaoxin Lingzhi Venture Capital Partnership (Limited Partnership) invested RMB 20 million in the angel round; its stake is about 9.17%, and the valuation at that time was only RMB 100 million. In January 2026, Zhejiang Venture Capital Group, under Zhejiang State-Owned Capital Operation Co., Ltd., invested RMB 90 million through two funds, Hangzhou Zhechuang Baige and Jinhua Zhechuang Jinyi Zhikong, pushing the valuation to RMB 1.09 billion.

Strong racing results underpin the high valuation. In 2026, ZX Moto entered the Superbike World Championship's WorldSSP class as a manufacturer. In March, Valentin Debise won both Portuguese races on its 820RR. Further wins followed in Hungary, the Czech Republic, and Italy; by June 13, the team had six season victories.

On finances, Zhang told the 2026 partner conference that 2025 output value reached RMB 750 million, R&D spending RMB 69.58 million, and R&D as a share of sales 9.33%, with a loss of RMB 22.78 million.

The company has recently accelerated capacity expansion. According to Liangjiang New Area, it spent RMB 48.48 million on production-base land there in June, then signed with CMCU Engineering in July to build a local R&D and production base with planned annual capacity of 500,000 motorcycles. Zhang previously said June production exceeded 10,000.

CCTV News also reported that TÜV Rheinland Greater China, an independent German testing, inspection, and certification organization, recently announced that ZX Moto's 500RR had received an EU Whole Vehicle Type Approval (WVTA) certificate from Luxembourg's transport ministry. It is the company's first such certificate and confirms the model meets EU market-access requirements, allowing lawful entry into EU member states and other jurisdictions recognizing WVTA.
