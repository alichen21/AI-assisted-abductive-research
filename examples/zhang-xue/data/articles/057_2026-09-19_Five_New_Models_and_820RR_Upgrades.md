## ZX Moto Unveils Two Upgraded Championship Models at Motorcycle Expo, Both over RMB 100,000; Zhang Says Annual Production and Sales Could Reach 100,000

Jimu News reporter Deng Bo

On September 19 at the China Motorcycle Expo in Chongqing, ZX Moto held a launch presenting two new models, the 600V cruiser and 820X adventure bike, plus three upgrades: the 2027 500F retro street bike and the championship 820RR's track version, 820RR-RC, and full-carbon-fiber version, 820RR-RS. The latter two cost RMB 128,000 and RMB 138,000 respectively.

ZX Moto's exhibition stand. Photo by Jimu News reporter Deng Bo.

Zhang said that since winning WSBK races from March 2026 onward, support and attention from across society had produced a huge change in sales. The original 2026 target was 50,000 units in production and sales, with a stretch target of 60,000. That had already been achieved in August; the year's figure could reach 100,000. "Beyond sales, it has given our team courage, and even given China's motorcycle industry courage."

He also said that in 2027, alongside the motorcycle world championship, ZX Moto would enter two other top events: the motocross world championship and the rally world championship, aiming for a stage victory in 2027. "Each competition costs RMB 20–30 million. Only by entering an arena full of experts can we force ourselves to grow rapidly, identify and solve problems in competition, and gradually catch leading brands—even surpass them."

The major new launch was the 820X adventure bike. Zhang said it offers almost every smart feature available on comparable motorcycles and could be called a torque monster. Touring and Assault versions cost RMB 57,800 and RMB 46,800 respectively. The new 600V cruiser costs RMB 27,800.

The company also introduced two upgrades of the WSBK-winning 820RR: the full-carbon-fiber 820RR-RS at RMB 138,000 and the track-focused 820RR-RC at RMB 128,000. Zhang said the RC differs very little from the winning race bike, while the full-carbon version has equipment comparable to an international-brand model costing RMB 1.5 million.
