# Zhang Xue English source data

The supplied collection contains 29 archived news files about Zhang Xue, Kove, and ZX Moto: 28 English translations and one originally English-language article (042). This count refers to the supplied collection, not the coverage of every research run.

- [Browse the English articles and source index](INDEX.md).
- [Open the individual article files](articles/).
- [Download the source metadata as CSV](source-index.csv).
- [Read the source metadata as JSON](source-index.json).

The metadata preserves archive IDs, English titles, publishers, publication and retrieval dates, original and final URLs, collection scope, and SHA-256 hashes of the supplied English files. Hashes identify the supplied files; they do not verify the truth of the reports. Translations preserve the boundaries of the available source material, including partial texts. The sources have not been independently reverified for this repository release.

The original files describe their collection scope as "user-downloaded full text for private research." The collection owner has confirmed the right to make these 29 supplied files public and authorized their inclusion in this repository. The original collection-scope label is retained as provenance; it describes the earlier collection context. The repository license does not grant rights to third-party news articles or their translations.
